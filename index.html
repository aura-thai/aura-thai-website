Family-run since 2011.

Aura Thai has been serving Bixby Knolls out of the same corner on Atlantic Avenue since 2011 — scratch curries, wok dishes cooked to order, and noodle soups that taste like they came out of someone’s home kitchen, because they did.

Most of what we make goes out the door as takeout, so the whole menu is built to travel well without losing what makes it good — and we still cook every order fresh when it comes in.
